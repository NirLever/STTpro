Refund Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

We want you to be completely satisfied with STTpro. That is why we offer a straightforward 30-day money-back guarantee on all license purchases. If STTpro does not meet your needs, we will refund your purchase — no questions asked.

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with STTpro for any reason, you may request a full refund within 30 days of your original purchase date. This guarantee applies to:

  • First-time license purchases (monthly or annual plans).
  • Upgrades to a higher-tier plan (refund covers the upgrade cost).

The 30-day period begins on the date the payment was processed, as shown on your purchase receipt.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, simply send an email to support@sttpro.com with the following information:

  • The email address associated with your STTpro account.
  • Your license key or order number (found in your purchase confirmation email).
  • A brief reason for the refund (optional, but helps us improve the product).

You do not need to provide a detailed justification. We honor all refund requests made within the 30-day window.

3. Processing Time

Once we receive your refund request, here is what to expect:

  • Acknowledgment: We will confirm receipt of your request within 1 business day.
  • Processing: Refunds are typically processed within 3–5 business days.
  • Payment return: Depending on your payment provider (credit card, PayPal, etc.), it may take an additional 5–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.

4. After the Refund

Once a refund is processed:

  • Your STTpro license key will be deactivated.
  • You will lose access to the licensed features of the software.
  • You may continue to use the free trial version if the trial period has not expired.
  • Any data stored locally on your computer (settings, transcript history) will not be deleted automatically. You can uninstall STTpro at your discretion.

5. Conditions & Exceptions

While we aim to be as generous as possible, the following conditions apply:

  • One refund per customer: The 30-day money-back guarantee applies once per customer. If you purchase again after receiving a refund, subsequent purchases are not eligible for another refund under this policy.
  • Subscription renewals: For recurring subscriptions, refund requests must be made within 30 days of the specific renewal charge you wish to dispute. We recommend cancelling your subscription before the renewal date if you no longer wish to use the software.
  • Abuse: We reserve the right to deny refund requests if we detect patterns of abuse, such as repeated purchases and refunds.

6. Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your Account page or by contacting us at support@sttpro.com. When you cancel:

  • Your license remains active until the end of your current billing period.
  • You will not be charged again after cancellation.
  • Cancelling is not the same as requesting a refund. If you want a refund for the current period, please submit a separate refund request.

7. OpenAI API Costs

Please note that STTpro refunds cover only the STTpro license fee. Any charges incurred on your OpenAI account for API usage (Whisper transcription, GPT Smart Prompts) are billed directly by OpenAI and are not covered by this refund policy. For questions about OpenAI billing, please contact OpenAI Support.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our refund policy or need help with your request, reach out to us:

support@sttpro.com

We typically respond within 1 business day.

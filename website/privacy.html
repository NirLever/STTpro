Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

At STTpro, we believe your privacy is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We designed STTpro with a privacy-first approach — your audio, transcriptions, and API keys never pass through our servers.

1. Data Collection

STTpro collects minimal data necessary to operate the software and provide you with a license. Specifically:

  • Account information: When you purchase a license, we collect your email address and the payment information you provide at checkout. Payment processing is handled by our third-party payment provider; we do not store your full credit card details.
  • License data: We store your license key and associated email address to validate your subscription and manage your account.
  • Basic usage analytics: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics such as application version, operating system version, and feature usage frequency. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

STTpro is designed so that sensitive data stays on your machine:

  • Audio recordings: We never receive, store, or have access to your audio recordings. Audio is sent directly from your computer to OpenAI's API and is not retained by STTpro.
  • Transcriptions: Your transcription text is generated by the OpenAI Whisper API and returned directly to your computer. We never see or store your transcriptions.
  • Keystrokes or screen content: STTpro does not log keystrokes, capture screenshots, or monitor your screen content.

3. API Key Storage

Your OpenAI API key is stored locally on your computer using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). DPAPI encrypts the key using your Windows user credentials, meaning only your Windows account can decrypt it. Your API key is never transmitted to STTpro servers and is used solely to authenticate requests from your machine directly to OpenAI.

4. Audio Processing

When you use STTpro to transcribe speech, the following occurs:

  • Audio is captured from your selected microphone device.
  • The audio is sent directly from your computer to OpenAI's Whisper API over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • OpenAI processes the audio and returns the transcription to your computer.
  • The audio data is not stored locally after the transcription is complete (unless you have explicitly enabled a recording feature).

OpenAI's handling of audio data is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy. As of the time of writing, OpenAI states that data submitted via the API is not used to train their models. We encourage you to review OpenAI's policies directly.

5. Analytics

We may use lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how STTpro is used in aggregate. This helps us prioritize features and fix bugs. Analytics data is anonymous and includes:

  • Application version and operating system version
  • Feature usage counts (e.g., how often Smart Prompts are used)
  • Error and crash reports (without personal data)

You can opt out of analytics at any time in STTpro's Settings. No analytics data includes audio content, transcription text, or personally identifiable information.

6. Cookies

The STTpro website uses minimal cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for account login and session management on our website.
  • Analytics cookies: We may use privacy-focused analytics (such as Plausible or similar) that do not track you across websites and do not use personal identifiers.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to third-party advertisers.

7. Third-Party Services

STTpro integrates with the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI API: For Whisper speech-to-text and GPT-based Smart Prompts. Data is sent directly from your computer to OpenAI.
  • Payment processor: For handling purchases and subscriptions. We use industry-standard payment providers that are PCI-DSS compliant.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data we hold:

  • All communications between your computer and our servers use TLS encryption.
  • API keys are encrypted locally with DPAPI.
  • We follow security best practices for our website and backend infrastructure.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of analytics data collection.
  • Update or correct your account information.

10. Children's Privacy

STTpro is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify users via email or an in-app notification. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:
